Importing a menu with quoted bold categories and product names in table headers
now preserves the categories, product names and their quantity prices.

- Quantity rows such as `100qty | $100` stay attached to the named product.
- Simple price tables fit the phone width, with prices aligned right.
- Ordinary quoted text and gallery layouts retain their previous behavior.

Install the attached APK over your existing app. To test the corrected import,
paste the original menu again as a new page. Previously imported pages are not
rewritten automatically.

The broader import correction panel remains planned. This release fixes the
supplied category and quantity-table example.
